🚨 The Problem

Safety emergencies are unpredictable—and response is often delayed

  • ●
    Crimes & Accidents Rising: Medical emergencies and road accidents are increasing daily.
  • ●

  • ●
    Tech Failure: Existing safety apps fail completely without internet connectivity.
  • ●
    No Central Intelligence: Emergency services lack real-time, centralized coordination data.

    High Risk Groups: Vulnerable groups (women, children, elderly) remain most exposed.

Slide 5 - How It Works (Emergency Flow)

🔄 How It Works (Emergency Flow)

1
Trigger SOS

User presses one-touch button or hardware device triggers SOS.

2
Network Selection

System auto-selects best available channel (GSM, LTE, or fallback).

3
Classification

AI classifies incident type and severity for appropriate response.

4
Multi-Alerts

Alerts sent to:

  • Trusted Contacts
  • Community
  • Police / EMS
5
Resolution

Live tracking & coordination until the user is safe.
